## Your laptop and LockDown Browser

Set up your laptop by the date in the [Overview](#overview):

1. Use a Windows or Mac laptop. LockDown Browser does not run on Chromebooks, iPads, phones or
   Linux.
2. Install LockDown Browser from the
   [PrairieTest download page](https://us.prairietest.com/pt/docs/students/respondus-lockdown-browser),
   even if you have it from another course.
3. Test it with the optional [Pre-lab 9 LockDown Browser check](labs.md#the-pre-lab).

On exam day, restart your laptop and bring it fully charged, with its charger. Every seat has power.

### Borrowing a laptop <!-- {docsify-ignore} -->

If you do not have a Windows or Mac laptop, borrow one from UBC Library:

- Irving K. Barber Learning Centre, Chapman Learning Commons Help Desk, Level 3
- Koerner Library, Circulation Desk, Entrance Level
- Woodward Library, Information Desk, Entrance Level
- David Lam Library, Circulation Desk, Entrance Level

Ask the desk how long you can keep it and whether you can install LockDown Browser on it. See
[Technology Borrowing](https://services.library.ubc.ca/computers-technology/technology-borrowing/).

## How to prepare

- Review all three parts of the course. The final connects topics from the whole term
- Practise explaining ideas in your own words, including the decisions in your projects
- Sleep, eat and plan your route
- Pack your student ID, laptop and charger the night before

UBC has tips on [sleep](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/sleep-success) and
[stress](https://students.ubc.ca/health/health-topics/understanding-stress/).

## Centre for Accessibility arrangements

If you write with the Centre for Accessibility:

- Book your sitting with the Centre, as the
  [syllabus](syllabus.md#academic-accommodations-and-the-centre-for-accessibility) describes
- Confirm your arrangement with us 10 days before the exam, by email to the course admin address on
  [Course Communication](communication.md)
- You write on the Centre's computers, not your own laptop

If you do not confirm your arrangement, you write with the rest of the class, with no
accommodations.

## If you cannot attend

If an emergency or a serious, unexpected situation stops you from writing the exam:

1. Contact your faculty advising office as soon as you can.
2. Follow the
   [academic concession](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/academic-learning-resources/academic-concessions/)
   process.
3. Email the course staff address on [Course Communication](communication.md). Include your name,
   student number, what is happening, and whether you have contacted your advising office.

> [!ATTENTION]
> For a deferred exam, you must contact [UBC Science](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising)
> within 48 hours of the missed exam.

Also email us if you might be late. The sooner you contact us, the more options there may be.

More: [Science concession FAQ](https://science.ubc.ca/students/concession),
[Science exam FAQ](https://science.ubc.ca/students/advising/exams),
[concession form](https://parsa-rajabi.github.io/CPSC-100/assets/academic-concession.pdf),
[syllabus](syllabus.md#academic-concessions).

## Exam viewing and regrading

You can view your exam in person with a member of the teaching team, on a date we will announce. Do
not take photos or copies of any part of it.

Regrade requests are open only during a window we announce. After it closes, your grade is final.
See the [remarking policy](syllabus.md#remarking-policy).

To dispute your final standing in the course, talk to your instructor first. You can then apply for
a [Review of Assigned Standing](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/campus-wide-policies-and-regulations/review-assigned-standing).
Details are on the [UBC grades page](https://students.ubc.ca/enrolment/courses/grades/).

## Help and mental health support

If you feel overwhelmed, distressed, unwell or unsafe, ask for help early. You do not need to wait
until things are severe.

| Resource | When to use it |
| :------- | :------------- |
| [UBC Counselling Services](https://students.ubc.ca/health/counselling-services/) | Stress, anxiety, test anxiety, or deciding what support you need |
| [Here2Talk](https://here2talk.ca/) | Free 24/7 counselling by phone, app or chat |
| [UBC Student Assistance Program](https://students.ubc.ca/health/ubc-student-assistance-program-sap) | Free 24/7 wellness support |
| [UBC Student Health Service](https://students.ubc.ca/health/student-health-service) | Medical or health concerns |
| [UBC Health and Wellbeing](https://students.ubc.ca/health/) | Health, wellbeing, peer support and self-help |
| [Syllabus: Wellness and Mental Health](syllabus.md#wellness-and-mental-health) | The course list of resources |

> [!WARNING]
> If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
