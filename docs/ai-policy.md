# Artificial Intelligence Use Policy

> [!NOTE]
> **This policy has not been published yet.** It will be developed with the class during
> an in-class activity early in the term, and posted here and on the
> [syllabus](syllabus.md#artificial-intelligence-ai-policy) before the add/drop deadline.

## Why This Policy Is Not Here Yet

CPSC 100 is a course about how computing shapes the world you live in, and generative AI is one of the clearest live examples of that. Students in this course are among the people most directly affected by how AI tools are used for learning, so it makes more sense to write this policy **with** you than to hand it to you on the first day.

## How the Policy Will Be Developed

- **In-class activity.** Early in the term we will run a class activity in which students identify which uses of AI tools they consider acceptable in this course and which they consider unacceptable, along with the reasoning behind each.
- **Synthesis.** The teaching team will consolidate the class's input into a single policy, resolving conflicts and making sure the result is consistent with UBC, Faculty of Science, and Computer Science department policy.
- **Publication.** The resulting policy will be posted on this page and linked from the [syllabus](syllabus.md#artificial-intelligence-ai-policy).

## When It Will Be Available

The policy will be published **before the add/drop deadline**, so that you can read the finalized policy while you still have the option to drop the course.

## Your Agreement

**By remaining enrolled in this course after the policy is published, you agree to abide by it.** If, after reading the finalized policy, you decide the terms do not work for you, the add/drop deadline is your opportunity to withdraw.

## Until Then

Until the course policy is published, the university's expectations still apply. Students are responsible for being familiar with and adhering to the [UBC Academic Integrity guidelines](https://learningcommons.ubc.ca/academic-support/academic-integrity-citations/) and the [Academic Integrity](syllabus.md#academic-integrity) section of the syllabus.

## Questions

If you have questions about AI use before the policy is published, ask on the course discussion board or bring them to the in-class activity. That is the right place for them, and questions raised there will shape the policy.
